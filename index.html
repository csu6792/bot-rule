比賽方法與操作流程 Competition Methods and Operation Flow

01

隨機抽籤與出發 Random Draw and Start

參賽者需 隨機抽出 4 個 軌道模組，自行決定擺放順序後將機器人放到軌道上出發。 Participants must randomly draw 4 track modules, decide their placement order, and place the robot on the track to start.

02

後換前 無限循環 Move Back to Front / Infinite Loop

當機器人完全離開後方某段軌道時，選手可將該軌道拼接至 最前方 讓路徑延伸。 When the robot completely leaves a rear track section, players can connect it to the very front to extend the path.

!

禁止觸碰機器人 Strictly Do Not Touch the Robot

機器人運行中禁止接觸機器人！一旦觸碰即視為失誤，須申請重置且消耗次數。 Touching the robot during operation is prohibited! Any contact is deemed a mistake, requiring a reset and consuming an attempt.

Critical Opportunity Limit

機會限制：2 次重置 Attempt Limit: 2 Resets

每場比賽僅有 3 次啟動機會。凡發生掉落、越界、或人為觸碰機器人，皆需申請重置。 Each match allows only 3 start attempts. Any falls, boundary violations, or human contacts require a reset. 當第 3 次啟動發生失誤，計時器將即刻停止並終止比賽。 If a mistake occurs on the 3rd attempt, the timer stops immediately and terminate the match.

01

機器人掉落 Robot Fall

機器人發生墜落時，選手必須手動將其搬回起點。且 所有軌道模組必須拿起 並重新擺放。 When the robot falls, players must manually return it to the start. In addition, all track modules must be picked up and rearranged.

02

軌道越界 Track Out of Bounds

拼接過程中，若軌道任何部分 碰到或越過紅色警戒線，該次嘗試即刻判定失敗，需申請重置並計入次數。 During splicing, if any part of the track touches or crosses the red boundary line, the attempt fails immediately, requiring a counted reset.
